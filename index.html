My Projects

Powerlifting Tracker

As a competitive powerlifter, I had no simple way to track powerlifting sessions, sets, reps, and effort levels, so I built a web app solo, using AI as a learning aid to work through unfamiliar concepts for my first full-stack project. The app enables users to easily record training sessions and blocks while tracking progress toward their strength goals over time. A key technical challenge was implementing an authentication system (JWT + user-scoped data) to ensure sessions, sets, and weigh-ins stay correctly tied to the right user. Today, the app is live, fully deployed, and actively used for my own personal training.
